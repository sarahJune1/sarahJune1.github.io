### Overview
Block Party is a civic technology platform designed to engage the public in local policy discussions by making the information more accessible.

### What It Does
- Creates a transcript and autogenerated summary from the meeting recording
- Archive of NYC contextual data from 2016 to present day
- Makes public participation more accessible

### Impact & Reach
- Used by community members and organizers
- Featured in civic technology and media spaces
- Supported conversations around public participation and democracy

### Publicity & Recognition
Block Party has been featured and discussed across multiple platforms and events.

- Cornell Tech Public Interest Technology  
- MLOps Showcase  
- NYU Center for Urban Science and Progress
